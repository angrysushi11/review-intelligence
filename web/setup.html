Technical details and data limits

Review Intel retrieves public App Store and Google Play reviews. It does not sign in to App Store Connect or Play Console, and it cannot change store data.

MCP endpoint
https://www.willthiseverwork.com/review-intel/mcp
Per response
Up to 500 reviews. Google Play can continue into later batches; Apple is limited by its public storefront feed.
Storage
No review database is present in the application. Ordinary infrastructure request logs may still exist, so do not send credentials or private exports.
Evidence limit
Reviews reveal patterns and language. They do not prove revenue, retention, conversion, or causality on their own.
