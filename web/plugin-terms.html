Plugin terms. Use the analysis as evidence, not certainty.

These terms cover the skills-only Review Intelligence package, which analyzes review exports you supply in an OpenAI product.

The short version

  • You provide the review data and must have the right to use it.
  • The plugin analyzes only the sample you supply.
  • Findings support investigation and testing, not guaranteed business results.
  • Your OpenAI service and workspace terms also apply.

On this page

Scope

Review Intelligence is a DoubleDash analysis method delivered in this skills-only OpenAI package. It organizes and analyzes the review text and details you paste or upload in the chat. It makes no live requests and connects to no DoubleDash server.

The separate GitHub package for Codex also includes Review Intel’s public read-only MCP connection. The website and MCP privacy policy covers that connection.

Your content and responsibilities

You’re responsible for the content you supply, the instructions you give and the decisions you make from the output. Use only data you’re permitted to process. Don’t upload credentials, private developer-console data, unlawfully obtained material or personal information the analysis doesn’t need.

Your use of ChatGPT or Codex stays subject to the terms, usage policies and workspace rules of your OpenAI service.

Evidence limits

The plugin can report patterns only in the sample you supplied and it could parse. Exports can be selected, incomplete, duplicated, filtered or missing context. Reviews and analysis don’t by themselves establish demand, revenue, retention, conversion, causality, roadmap priority or the views of every user.

Check consequential conclusions against product analytics, research, legal requirements and other suitable evidence before you act.

Data handling

The skills-only package runs inside the OpenAI host and doesn’t send what you supply to DoubleDash. The plugin privacy policy lists the data, purposes, recipients, retention and controls.

Availability and changes

The plugin and its method may change, pause or become unavailable. Outputs can contain mistakes, so review them for your intended use. These terms may be updated when the package or its data flow changes; the date at the top identifies this version.
