Use the analysis as evidence, not certainty.

These terms cover the skills-only Review Intelligence plugin, which analyzes review exports you supply in ChatGPT or Codex.

The short version

  • You provide the review data and must have the right to use it.
  • The plugin analyzes only the sample you supply.
  • Findings support investigation and testing, not guaranteed business outcomes.
  • Your OpenAI service and workspace terms also apply.

Scope

Review Intelligence is a DoubleDash analysis method delivered as a skills-only plugin. It organizes and analyzes review text and metadata you paste or upload in the host conversation. It performs no live retrieval or DoubleDash server connection.

Your content and responsibilities

You are responsible for the content you supply, the instructions you give, and the decisions you make from the output. Use only data you are permitted to process. Do not upload credentials, private developer-console data, unlawfully obtained material, or personal information that is unnecessary for the analysis.

Your use of ChatGPT or Codex remains subject to the terms, usage policies, and workspace rules that apply to your OpenAI service.

Evidence limits

The plugin can report patterns only in the supplied and successfully parsed sample. Exports can be selected, incomplete, duplicated, filtered, or missing context. Reviews and analysis do not by themselves establish demand, revenue, retention, conversion, causality, roadmap priority, or the views of every user.

Check consequential conclusions against product analytics, research, legal requirements, and other appropriate evidence before acting.

Data handling

The plugin runs within the OpenAI host and does not send your supplied data to DoubleDash. Read the plugin privacy policy for the data categories, purposes, recipients, retention boundary, and controls.

Availability and changes

The plugin and its analysis method may change, pause, or become unavailable. Outputs may contain mistakes and should be reviewed for the intended use. These terms may be updated when the package or its data flow changes; the date above identifies this version.

Questions about these terms: tools@doubledash.me. For usage help, see plugin support.
