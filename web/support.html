Check the coverage

Store feeds can skip reviews or stop paging. If an answer looks incomplete, check its source, country, sort order, review count and coverage before you draw a conclusion.

Apple and Google control these feeds, so the sample can change.

Quick fixes. Try these before you write.

  • The App Store gave me 0 or about 10 reviews.

    Apple’s public feed is flaky. Try again in a minute, or pick another country.

  • Copy didn’t work.

    Press Copy again, or download the .md file and upload it to your chat.

  • I need more than 500 reviews.

    Connect Claude or Codex. On Google Play it keeps collecting the next batch; Apple’s feed stops earlier.

Evidence limits are in the terms, data handling in the privacy policy.
