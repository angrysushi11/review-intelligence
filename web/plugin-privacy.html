Skills-only plugin · last updated 1 October 2026

A clear boundary for supplied reviews.

Review Intelligence is a set of analysis instructions that runs inside the OpenAI host you selected. It analyzes only reviews you paste or upload and performs no live retrieval.

The short version

  • You choose which review data to supply.
  • Your selected OpenAI product or workspace hosts the conversation and analysis.
  • No review data is sent to a DoubleDash MCP server or other DoubleDash service.
  • DoubleDash retains no copy of your plugin inputs or results.

Data the plugin can process

The plugin can process the review export and instructions you provide. Depending on your file, that may include review text, ratings, dates, app versions, developer replies, app or store labels, and public reviewer names or identifiers. It can also process your questions and produce an analysis in the conversation.

File names, file metadata, account information, and ordinary service metadata may be handled by OpenAI as the host under its own policies. The plugin does not need store credentials, API keys, private developer-console access, or payment information. Do not supply them.

Why the data is used

The supplied material is used to parse and organize reviews, identify evidence-backed patterns and customer language, compare labelled review sets, answer your question, and show the limits of the available sample. The plugin itself sends none of that content to DoubleDash.

Who receives the data

The selected OpenAI host processes the content needed to run the conversation and return the analysis according to the product, account, and workspace settings that apply to you. If you use a managed workspace, its administrators may control or have access to data according to the workspace agreement and settings.

The skills-only plugin makes no outbound request to DoubleDash, so DoubleDash does not receive your review export, prompt, or result through the plugin.

Retention

The plugin creates no DoubleDash-held copy of its inputs or results because it does not send them to DoubleDash. This statement does not cover information you choose to send separately by email.

OpenAI controls retention for content held by ChatGPT or Codex. The applicable period depends on the product, account or workspace, settings, and actions such as deleting a chat or file. Review OpenAI’s current chat and file retention policy rather than relying on a fixed period stated here.

Your controls

Supply only reviews you are permitted to use. Remove unnecessary personal, confidential, or sensitive information before uploading. You can keep identifiers out of the export when they are not needed for the analysis.

Use the controls available in your OpenAI account or workspace to manage conversations, files, model-improvement preferences, exports, and deletion. OpenAI explains the current options in Data controls in ChatGPT. Available controls can vary by plan and workspace.

Contact

For questions about the Review Intelligence plugin, you may email tools@doubledash.me. Details you voluntarily include in that email are received by DoubleDash through a separate support channel. Do not attach a review export or include personal, confidential, or sensitive review data. For requests about content held by ChatGPT or Codex, use the controls and contact routes provided by OpenAI.
