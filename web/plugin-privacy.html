Review Intelligence plugin. Updated 1 October 2026.

Plugin privacy. Nothing you supply through it reaches DoubleDash.

Review Intelligence is a set of analysis instructions that runs inside the OpenAI product you chose. It analyzes only the reviews you paste or upload, and fetches nothing.

The short version

  • You choose which review data to supply.
  • Your OpenAI product or workspace hosts the chat and the analysis.
  • No review data goes to a DoubleDash MCP server or any other DoubleDash service.
  • DoubleDash keeps no copy of your plugin inputs or results.

What it can process

The review export and the instructions you provide. Depending on your file, that can include review text, ratings, dates, app versions, developer replies, app or store labels, and public reviewer names or identifiers. It also processes your questions and writes the analysis in the chat.

OpenAI, as the host, may handle file names, file metadata, account information and ordinary service metadata under its own policies. The plugin doesn’t need store credentials, API keys, developer-console access or payment details. Don’t supply them.

Why it’s used

To parse and organize the reviews, find evidence-backed patterns and customer language, compare labelled sets, answer your question and show the limits of the sample. The plugin itself sends none of it to DoubleDash.

Who receives it

The OpenAI host processes what it needs to run the chat and return the analysis, under the product, account and workspace settings that apply to you. In a managed workspace, its administrators may control or access data under the workspace agreement and settings.

The plugin makes no outbound request to DoubleDash, so DoubleDash doesn’t receive your export, prompt or result through it.

Retention

Because nothing is sent to DoubleDash, the plugin creates no DoubleDash-held copy of its inputs or results. This doesn’t cover anything you choose to send separately by email.

OpenAI controls retention for content in ChatGPT or Codex. The period depends on the product, your account or workspace, your settings and actions such as deleting a chat or file. Check OpenAI’s current chat and file retention policy rather than relying on a fixed period stated here.

Your controls

Supply only reviews you’re permitted to use. Remove unnecessary personal, confidential or sensitive information before uploading, and leave identifiers out of the export when the analysis doesn’t need them.

Use your OpenAI account or workspace controls to manage chats, files, model-improvement preferences, exports and deletion. OpenAI explains the current options in Data controls in ChatGPT. Controls can vary by plan and workspace.

Contact

Questions about the plugin: tools@doubledash.me. Anything you put in that email reaches DoubleDash through a separate support channel, so don’t attach a review export or include personal, confidential or sensitive review data. For content held in ChatGPT or Codex, use OpenAI’s controls and contact routes.
