Question the evidence like a conversation

Reviews are not a live user interview, and they cannot answer a follow-up. They are public, first-person evidence from people who tried the product. A connected analysis workflow lets you question that corpus and trace each answer back to the language users actually chose.

Start with a public review packet

  1. Paste the public listing. Use an Apple App Store or Google Play URL.
  2. Choose the country. Reviews differ by storefront, so select the market you actually want to inspect.
  3. Retrieve and continue. Copy or download the Markdown, or connect the MCP and analyze inside the conversation.

What the review packet includes

The packet records the app, platform, selected country, source, pages fetched, requested limit, review count, available date range, and rating mix. Individual entries include the public fields the store supplies, such as rating, date, title, text, version, language, and reviewer name.

What analysis can and cannot tell you

Review evidence can expose patterns, exact customer language, and questions worth testing. It cannot prove revenue, retention, conversion impact, or the views of every user without other data.

Review Intelligence FAQ

Do I need App Store Connect or Google Play Console?

No. Review Retriever uses public store listings, so it does not need a developer account, store-console access, or an API key.

Can I retrieve more than 500 reviews?

Yes, through the connected Google Play workflow. The MCP returns up to 500 reviews per response, then supplies a continuation cursor so Claude Cowork or Codex can request the next batch until Google Play returns no further page. Apple can be queried across storefronts, but its public feeds do not expose the same continuation. Store availability still controls coverage, so this is not a promise of every review ever posted.

What file format do I get?

The web tool exports Markdown. You can copy the result or download a .md file. It does not currently export CSV, Excel, or JSON from this page.

Can I analyze reviews for a competitor’s app?

Yes, if the app has a supported public App Store or Google Play listing. The same limits and storefront boundaries apply.

Is this an app review scraper?

People often use that phrase for a public review exporter. Review Retriever retrieves public written reviews from supported store listings; it does not sign in to or extract private developer-console data.

What changes when I connect the MCP?

The web page gives you a Markdown export. The MCP lets Claude Cowork or Codex retrieve structured review records inside the conversation, continue through additional Google Play batches, and use the Review Intelligence skill for specific follow-up questions.

What can I ask Review Intelligence?

Ask what users love or hate, where expectations break, which phrases repeat, what creates trust or payment friction, and where a competitor leaves an opening. The useful answer should show the review evidence and label inference rather than pretending the sample proves everything.
