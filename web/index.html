    Questions. Anything else? Email me.

    Do I need App Store Connect or Google Play Console?

    No. Review Intel reads public store listings, so you need no developer account, console access or API key.

    Can I get more than 500 reviews?

    On Google Play, yes: connect Claude or Codex and it keeps collecting the next batch until the store runs out. Apple’s public feed stops earlier. No public source can promise every review ever posted.

    Does it work on a competitor’s app?

    Yes, and on any app with a public App Store or Google Play listing: a similar app, an adjacent one, or the workaround people use today.

    The App Store gave me only a few reviews. Why?

    Apple’s public review feed is unreliable. If you get 0 or about 10 reviews, try again in a minute or pick another country.

    What file format do I get?

    Markdown. Copy & open Claude copies it with your question, or you can copy it yourself or download the .md file. There’s no CSV, Excel or JSON export.

    Is this an app review scraper?

    People often call it that. Review Intel reads public written reviews from store listings. It doesn’t sign in anywhere or touch private developer-console data.

    What can I ask about the reviews?

    Best start

    • First useful read: the two or three things in these reviews you’d most likely miss, and why they matter.

    Before you build

    • What do users wish this app did?
    • Why do people switch apps?
    • Which users does it serve badly?

    Product

    • What keeps people coming back?
    • What breaks, and when do people give up?
    • Where does onboarding lose people?

    Money

    • Is it the price, or when the price appears?
    • What turns a trial into a refund request?
    • What makes people feel unsafe paying?

    Marketing

    • Which words do happy users use?
    • What should screenshots and ads say?
    • Which claims are safe to make?

    Support

    • Where does support fail?
    • What changed across versions or dates?
