Terms. Use the evidence with care.

Review Intel helps you look at public app reviews and decide what to investigate, build, improve, test or say.

Using Review Intel

Review Intel is a DoubleDash tool. It collects and organizes public App Store and Google Play reviews to help you research product experience, customer language and questions to test.

You’re responsible for the app links, markets, review files and instructions you provide, and for the decisions you make with the results.

Evidence limits

Review Intel reports patterns in the reviews it can collect. Apple, Google and other upstream services control which reviews are available, their order, their details and their coverage, and all of that can change. Results may be incomplete.

Reviews and analysis don’t establish demand, revenue, retention, causality or the views of every user. Check important conclusions against other evidence before you act.

Data and other services

Use only data you’re permitted to process. Don’t submit credentials, confidential exports or private personal information. Public review text and reviewer names can still be personal data.

Review Retriever, the part of Review Intel that collects reviews, processes a request to produce a response or a download. Its code has no review database or persistence layer. Hosting and store providers may process or keep request metadata under their own policies.

If you use a connected AI client, it may process the reviews and your instructions under its own terms and privacy policy. Review Retriever returns reviews, not an AI analysis. The privacy policy covers the website and the MCP server; the extension privacy policy covers the extension’s link hand-off.

Availability and changes

The tool may change, pause or become unavailable. These terms may be updated when the product changes; the date at the top identifies this version.
