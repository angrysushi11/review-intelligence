Use the evidence with care.

Review Intel helps you examine public app reviews and decide what to investigate, build, improve, test, or say.

The short version

  • Review Retriever collects a bounded sample of public written reviews.
  • Review patterns are evidence about that sample, not proof of demand or business outcomes.
  • Your AI client and upstream providers have their own terms and privacy policies.

Use of Review Intel

Review Intel is a DoubleDash tool. It retrieves and organizes publicly available App Store and Google Play reviews to help you research product experience, customer language, and questions to test. You are responsible for the app links, markets, review exports, and instructions you provide, and for decisions you make using the results.

Read about Review Intel or get support.

Evidence limits

The tool reports patterns in the reviews it can retrieve. Review availability, ordering, metadata, and coverage depend on Apple, Google, and other upstream services and may change. Results may be incomplete. Reviews and analysis do not establish demand, revenue, retention, causality, or the views of every user. Check important conclusions against other evidence before acting.

Data and third-party services

Use only data you are permitted to process. Do not submit credentials, confidential exports, or private personal information. Public review text and reviewer names can still be personal data. Review Retriever processes a request to produce a response or download and has no review database or persistence layer in its application source. Hosting and upstream store providers may process or retain request metadata under their own policies.

If you use a connected AI client, that client may process the reviews and instructions under its own terms and privacy policy. Review Retriever is the collection tool; it does not itself return an AI analysis. See the Review Intel privacy policy for website and MCP processing, or the extension-specific privacy policy for the extension's separate URL handoff.

Availability and changes

The tool may change, pause, or become unavailable. These terms may be updated when the product changes; the date above identifies this version.
