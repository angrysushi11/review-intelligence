Review Intelligence · skills-only plugin

See what users love, hate, and wish worked differently.

Review Intelligence analyzes app-review exports you paste or upload in ChatGPT or Codex, then shows what is worth investigating or testing next.

What it does

  • Works with review data you supply.
  • Finds evidence-backed themes, tensions, and customer language.
  • Keeps observations, inferences, and business claims separate.
  • Shows what the evidence supports and what still needs validation.

Start with your export

Paste reviews into the conversation or upload a review-export file supported by your OpenAI host. Ask a focused question, request a first useful read, or compare clearly labelled sets. The plugin analyzes only the material available in that conversation.

Questions the reviews can help answer

Find product friction and unmet needs; examine pricing, subscriptions, paywalls, billing, and cancellation; surface positioning, ASO, and creative directions in customers’ own language; or trace support failures, recovery, and trust concerns.

Evidence you can check

The analysis reports how many reviews were supplied, parsed, excluded, and analyzed. Percentages use the analyzed denominator. Findings cite stable review IDs, separate observations from inferences, and state coverage or claim limits when reviews cannot prove demand, revenue, retention, conversion, or causality.

Supplied reviews only

The skills-only plugin analyzes only reviews you paste or upload; it performs no live retrieval or DoubleDash server connection.
